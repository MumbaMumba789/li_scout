Find the lithium ground in Zambia that is about to open.

LiScout checks the Ministry of Mines licence register for ground listed for lithium-bearing pegmatite minerals, and shows which of it is lapsing, not in good standing, or still contested. Use it to decide where to apply or send a field team first.

1. Filter to lithium

Show only licences and applications that list lithium, tantalum, tin, beryllium, caesium, niobium or tourmaline with it.

2. Spot ground that may open

Flags licences that are non-compliant, pending renewal or transfer, or that expire within 12 months.

3. Take a shortlist to the field

Download the soonest-first list as a CSV with holder, dates and reasons, and check each one on the ground.

Who it is for

Small-scale prospectors and junior exploration geologists in Zambia who need to choose where to apply for ground and where to send a field team, without paying for a commercial mapping service.

A licence that lists lithium is not proof of a lithium deposit. Licences often list several minerals. Check the ground, the geology and the licence terms before you act. LiScout is a prototype, and it uses only the public register.

Data: Geological Survey of Zambia and Ministry of Mines cadastre (public WFS). Prototype.
